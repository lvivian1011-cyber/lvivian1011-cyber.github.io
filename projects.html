02 / Selected projects

Selected projects.

Research and design practice across community services, ageing, volunteer coordination and human–robot interaction.

Master’s thesis · Chongqing University · 2016

Urban Community Accessible Facilities Design Based on the Service Concept

Field research in three Chengdu community settings combined literature review, in-depth interviews and participant observation. Service touchpoint analysis informed redesign proposals for cultural life, health, transport and local commerce.

My role: project execution and thesis writing.

Master’s research extension · Chongqing University · 2016

Smart wristband for older adults

Interaction Design of a Smart Wristband for Urban Older Adults Living Without Their Children

Examined physiological and psychological needs to inform a wearable-device concept.

Principal Investigator · 2023–2025

Volunteer service platform

Design Research on a Volunteer Service Platform for Tianfu International Sports Events

Investigated organiser and volunteer needs through stakeholder research and service-journey analysis.

Participating researcher · 2025–2026

Interaction design for an inspection robot

AI visual inspection and robot design for cigarette box packaging

Conducted stakeholder research into user needs and operational requirements.

Professional design experience

Consultant Industrial Designer · Chengdu Wuyu Industrial Design Co., Ltd. · 2020–2024

Health, smart products & public facilities
