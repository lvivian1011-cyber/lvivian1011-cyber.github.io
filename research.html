01 / About & research

About Danping Wang.

Danping Wang is a Lecturer in Industrial Design at the School of Digital Intelligence Design, Chengdu Technological University.

She teaches undergraduate courses including Ergonomics, Smart Product Design, Design Psychology, and Service Design, and supervises the graduation projects of approximately seven undergraduate students each year.

Her research focuses on two areas: interdisciplinary curriculum reform and research in smart product design education; and participation in interdisciplinary design teams, including spatial visualization planning for production facilities and the design of AI-based quality inspection equipment for packaging plants.

She has also led several projects as principal investigator, including Design Research on the Volunteer Service Platform for Tianfu International Sports Events. The project uses service design and participatory co-design to improve volunteer services and support the smooth delivery of international events.

She holds an M.A. in Art and Design and a B.A. in Industrial Design from Chongqing University, and completed a government-sponsored exchange in Industrial Design at National Dong Hwa University.

Developing PhD interests

She is interested in how participatory design can shape digital support that respects older adults’ practical knowledge, preferences and control. This is a developing research direction, building on her background in industrial design, ergonomics and design psychology.

Context

Ageing & everyday life

Understanding when support feels welcome, unnecessary or intrusive within familiar activities.

Approach

Designing together

Exploring how co-design can connect people’s practical knowledge with concrete interaction decisions.

Question

Agency in interaction

Investigating how people might shape and revise when and how a digital assistant intervenes.
