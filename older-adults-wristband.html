Selected research & design projects

Smart wristband for older adults

Master’s research extension · Chongqing University · 2016

Interaction Design of a Smart Wristband for Urban Older Adults Living Without Their Children

  • Examined physiological and psychological needs to inform a wearable-device concept.
  • Developed a concept connecting older adults, the wristband and adult children, with proposed health-monitoring and alert functions.
  • Contributed to the co-authored design-study article in Packaging Engineering, 37(6), 79–82 (2016).
