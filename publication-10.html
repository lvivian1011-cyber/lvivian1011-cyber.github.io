Liti goucheng [Three-dimensional composition]

2022

Liu, Y., Deng, J., Zhao, L., Wang, D., Zhou, M., Zhou, X., Song, W., Hou, Y., Chen, J., Yuan, Y., Yin, C., & Yang, A. (Eds.).

Aviation Industry Press. ISBN 978-7-5165-2921-8.

Textbook · Co-editor

About the textbook

This application-oriented textbook introduces the fundamentals of three-dimensional composition through extensive hands-on practice. It is intended for art and design programmes and also serves as a reference for enthusiasts and professionals.

Contents at a glance

Chapter 1

Foundations of three-dimensional composition

Basic concepts, functions, historical development, structural elements and compositional forms.

Chapter 2

Practical creation

2.5D, aggregated and subtractive composition, alongside materials suitable for handcraft. The chapter explains the workflow of practical creation and the tools, ideas and concepts involved.

Chapter 3

Composition in design practice

Analysis of composition principles through architectural, spatial, product design and visual art cases.
